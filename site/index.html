Why trust the ranking

Real momentum

Stars gained this week from daily star counts (the top 1,000 repositories per language plus new ones); other repositories fall back to stars per week adjusted by their download trend.

Transparent score

Momentum 40%, maintenance 25%, adoption 25%, license 10%. Every part is in the answer, and the formula is open source.

No paid placement

Nobody can buy a better rank. The data is public GitHub, npm and PyPI data.
